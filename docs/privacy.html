Privacy Policy

Motiva — Daily quotes
Last updated: October 8, 2025

This policy describes how Motiva (“we”, “the app”) handles information when you use our iOS app.

What we collect

Motiva is designed to keep data on your device. We do not sell your personal information.

  • On your device: onboarding choices, favorite quotes, streak history, reminder times, and app preferences (stored locally).
  • Purchases: subscription status is processed by Apple and our subscription partner RevenueCat. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Notifications: if you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. Notification content may include quote text you already have in the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not use the App Tracking Transparency framework to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We do not collect contact lists, photos, or precise location for Motiva’s core features.

Third parties

  • Apple App Store — payments, subscriptions, and restore purchases.
  • RevenueCat — helps manage subscriptions and entitlement status (RevenueCat Privacy).

Your choices

  • Turn off notifications in iOS Settings or in the app.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Delete the app to remove locally stored data on that device.

Children

Motiva is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

Contact

Questions: support@motiva.app (replace with your support email before App Store submission).
